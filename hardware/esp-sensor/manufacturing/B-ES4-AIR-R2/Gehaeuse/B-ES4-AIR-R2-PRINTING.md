# B-ES4-AIR-R2 enclosure – Ultimaker S5

The R2 enclosure is a screw-fastened 150 x 120 mm housing for the 130 x
100 mm PCB. It adds labelled access for the COM/NO/NC push-in relay terminal
while retaining USB-C, field wiring, button and sensor ventilation openings.

Recommended first article settings:

- Ultimaker S5, AA 0.4 print core
- PLA, 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 5 top/bottom layers
- 25% gyroid infill
- 5 mm brim
- no supports for the supplied orientation
- four M3 x 16 case screws and four M3 x 6 PCB screws

Print `idm-roomsensor-b-es4-air-r2_S5-print-plate.stl` as one build plate or
print base and lid separately. Confirm USB-C, all push-in terminals, buttons,
LED windows, ventilation and relay clearance on the first article before a
larger production run.
