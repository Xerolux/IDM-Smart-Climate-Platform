# AIR-PRESSURE / BMP390 order package

Upload `AIR-PRESSURE-fabrication.zip`, then the BOM and CPL in `JLCPCB/`.
Select 4 layers, 1.6 mm FR-4, Standard PCBA, double-sided assembly and wave
soldering. U1/C5124834 is the BMP390; do not substitute it. J1/C58378 is the
bottom-side female AIR-SLOT socket and may require pre-ordering. Verify its
bottom-side placement and the pressure-port orientation in the JLCPCB viewer.
