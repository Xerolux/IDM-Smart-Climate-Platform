# AIR-CO2 / SCD41 order package

Upload `AIR-CO2-fabrication.zip`, then the BOM and CPL in `JLCPCB/`. Select
4 layers, 1.6 mm FR-4, Standard PCBA, double-sided assembly and wave soldering.
U1/C3659362 is the SCD41; do not substitute it. J1/C58378 is the bottom-side
female AIR-SLOT socket and may require pre-ordering. Verify its bottom-side
placement and the SCD41 port orientation in the JLCPCB viewer.
