# B-ES5-MODULAR enclosure – Ultimaker S5

- Material: PETG preferred for technical rooms; PLA for the first fit check
- Nozzle: AA 0.4 mm
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Walls: 4; top/bottom: 5 layers
- Infill: 25% gyroid
- Supports: off
- Adhesion: 5 mm brim
- Print the supplied S5 plate flat in its generated orientation

The plate is 310 x 120 mm before brim and fits the 330 x 240 mm Ultimaker S5
bed. Deburr ventilation slots without enlarging screw holes. Test-fit the bare
PCB and all desired modules before final assembly. The lid provides additional
height and internal guide rails for the three 25 x 25 mm modules.
